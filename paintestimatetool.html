How pricing works

Labor: quantity × rate. Labor discount is taken off labor only. Materials: quantity × your chosen customer charge per unit. Retail and contractor cost are private reference fields. Paint purchase tax = 7% × the paint purchase cost; it is tracked privately and not automatically added to the customer charge. Optional bid tax (when on) = 7% × discounted labor plus chargeable materials. Deposit is 50% of the tax-inclusive grand total, rounded to the nearest cent; balance is the remainder. Taxes and payment terms shown here are quote settings, not a determination of tax-law applicability.
